7. Backtest (price-only part of the rules)

Fixture mode: the series mix synthetic SoSoValue indices with real SoDEX prices, so these numbers only demonstrate the mechanics.

2026-09-21 to 2026-11-19 (59 days)ReturnMax drawdown
Proofdesk rules5.2%-2.6%
Equal-weight hold-18.5%-19.7%
BTC hold-28.5%-28.5%

5 rebalances, total turnover 1.8x NAV, costs 0.3% of NAV.

  • weekly rebalance (every 7 days), long-only, cash earns 0
  • costs 6.5 bps taker fee + 10 bps slippage on traded notional
  • regime proxy: share of assets with positive momentum (ETF flows, news and FOMC have no usable history)
  • SSI assets use the SoSoValue index series, not the SoDEX token price
  • only 59 trading days: this checks that the rules behave sanely, it is not evidence of alpha
  • the window is a market drawdown: any rule that sits in cash looks good here, so do not read the margin over the benchmarks as skill
